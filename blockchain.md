## Tooling

### HardHat ✅️

Hardhat is generally considered more flexible and developer-friendly, with superior debugging tools like
Solidity stack traces and native console.log support, a richer plugin system, and faster performance due to its integrated Hardhat Network for local development and mainnet forking.

### Truffle ❌️

Truffle is often seen as more opinionated, providing a comprehensive, out-of-the-box suite of tools including the Ganache GUI, and has been a staple in enterprise settings, though it may require more setup for tasks like blockchain forking compared to Hardhat's streamlined approach
